Two years ago we moved daily standup from a conference room to a video call. A few reflections now that it's been long enough to actually evaluate.

## What got better

Async written updates in the channel before the call means the call itself is shorter — ten minutes instead of twenty-five. People read what happened overnight before they show up.

## What got worse

Cross-team hallway conversations mostly didn't survive the move. We haven't found a substitute for the "oh, you're working on that too?" moments that used to happen by accident near the coffee machine.

## What we kept

On-call handoffs still happen synchronously, on video, every Monday. Some things are worth the extra ten minutes of a live conversation instead of a written note.
